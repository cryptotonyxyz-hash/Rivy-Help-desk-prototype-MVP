What I observed while testing (3 and 4 Nov 2026)
  • Android app v3.11.6: the date-of-birth picker blocked every date after 3 Nov 2004, typed dates were rejected, and the pencil icon did nothing. The website sign-up accepted 23 Nov 2004. Terms 3.2(a) give 18 as the minimum age.
  • Website dashboard showed an “account suspended” banner with a Contact Support button. I saw no reason or next step on the screen.
  • In-app chat: the first prompts asked for a “Referral”, then for background about me and my email, before the suspension was discussed.
  • EnergyStack: the 600W Jinko panel page shows 0 for efficiency, voltage rating, open circuit voltage and short circuit current, and a weight of 45 with no unit. The Geely Geometry A page has an empty Specifications section and shows 0 under Warranty, although its description lists specifications.
  • Terms: section 08 (Communication) opens with the same limitation-of-liability paragraph as section 06, which looks like a copy-paste slip.

These are what I saw, on the dates given. I do not know the reasons behind them, and my account may have been suspended for a reason I cannot see.
